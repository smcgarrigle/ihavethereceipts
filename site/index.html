What it does

From receipt photo to spending intelligence

Every feature below ships in the app today — this isn't a roadmap slide, it's what's running.

📄

Intelligent receipt processing

AI OCR extracts items, discounts, CRV/bottle deposits, and "2-for-$X" deals from photos or PDFs. A review screen lets you correct anything before it's saved, with bi-directional price math — edit quantity, total, or unit price and the others recalculate.

📊

Real analytics, not vanity charts

A dashboard of monthly spend and top categories, a trends page with per-item price history, and store-comparison pricing so you can see who actually sells your usual basket cheapest.

🧠

Smart item management

Fuzzy matching catches "Bananas" vs. "Banana Organic" as the same item, suggests merges, auto-tags categories, and remembers which suggestions you've already dismissed.

🥗

Nutrition & health insights

Items are enriched with Nutriscore, ingredients, and macronutrients via OpenFoodFacts and USDA FoodData Central — so your spending data doubles as a household nutrition log.

🔁

Restock predictions

A purchase-cadence engine learns how often you buy each item, predicts when you'll run out, and builds a store-grouped shopping list pointing at the cheapest place to buy it.

📱

Mobile-first, four themes

Fully responsive with a collapsible nav and touch-friendly controls, plus Dark, Light, Forest, and Sunset themes — all driven by a single CSS-variable design system.

Open source native

No black boxes, no required SaaS

Every layer of the stack is a well-known, inspectable open source project. The architecture treats self-hosting and data ownership as first-class requirements, not an afterthought.

✓

Built entirely on open tools

FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, Alembic, Pydantic, HTMX, Alpine.js, Tailwind, and Chart.js — every dependency is a project you can read the source of, not a managed API you have to trust blindly.

✓

Offline-first by design

All frontend dependencies are vendored locally under /static/js/vendor — zero CDN calls at runtime. A hardened Content-Security-Policy permits scripts and styles from 'self' only.

✓

No vendor lock-in on AI

Swap cloud OCR (Gemini 2.5 Flash) for a fully local vision model (IBM Granite 3.3, Qwen2-VL) via Ollama or LM Studio with one environment variable. Your receipts never have to leave your machine.

✓

Your data is a file, not a tenant row

The whole database is a single SQLite file. Copy it, back it up, open it with sqlite3 — there's no multi-tenant schema or managed cloud database standing between you and your own receipts.

✓

Documented dependencies

A maintained Software Bill of Materials tracks every dependency and its license, so "what's actually in this thing" is always a one-file answer.

✓

Runs on commodity hardware

Zero-config SQLite by default, single-user-first architecture, and local AI inference benchmarked down to a consumer GPU (RTX 2060) — no cluster required.
