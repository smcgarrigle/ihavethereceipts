How predictions work

The engine analyzes your purchase history to calculate average buying intervals. Items need at least 3 purchases to generate a prediction. Prices shown are the most recent price paid at each store. Items not purchased in 6+ months are considered stale and hidden from this view.

Click an item, or its Dismiss link, to stop being reminded about it for 1 week, 1 month or for good. This applies to the item at every store. Dismissed items are listed below the table and can be restored there.

API endpoints: /api/predictions/shopping-list (JSON) · /api/predictions/cadences (JSON)
